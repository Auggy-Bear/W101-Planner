Wizard101 Central - Pin property queries
=======================================

Base metadata for every query:
  ?Level  ?School  ?PowerPins  ?SwordPins  ?ShieldPins

Use format=csv and limit=5000. Save one page per effect.

Notes
-----
- There is no plain ?Critical, ?Damage, ?Resist, ?FlatDamage, ?FlatResist or
  ?CriticalBlock in your property list. Use the Global* and per-school forms.
- ?ArmorPiercing and ?PipConversion do exist as plain properties.
- ?PowerPins / ?SwordPins / ?ShieldPins identify pin shape; ?Level and ?School
  give the pin's school.
- Pin names alone do not carry school for universal pins; the School property is
  the reliable source.
- Accuracy and Outgoing Healing are the two gaps: no property with those names
  appears in the list, so those two need the test above or page-text parsing.
